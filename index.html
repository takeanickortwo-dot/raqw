📖 Prompt Writing Guide [click to toggle]

The Vibe Prompt Engine translates your words into montage rules — all in your browser.

⚡ Energy & Pace

  • hype fast chaos → short clips, 60fps, hard cuts
  • calm mellow slow → longer clips, 24fps, smooth
  • random mixed → random order, variable pacing

🎨 Aesthetic Keywords

  • vibrant colorful → saturated, high contrast
  • warm golden → warm tones, soft grain
  • dark gritty → crushed blacks, aggressive

🎵 Music Context

  • 120bpm → cut lengths match tempo
  • electronic upbeat → fast cuts on drops
  • Upload a track — auto-detects BPM, beats, and drops

🖼 Format Hints

  • vertical reel shorts → 9:16 output
  • youtube widescreen → 16:9 output

🎲 Randomization

  • random surprise → triggers full random config
  • Click Re-roll for a fresh take on the same prompt

🔥 Example Prompts

  • "psychonaut cliff jump stoner metal skater hype fast cuts vertical reel"
  • "calm nature documentary style with slow cinematic pacing"
  • "family road trip memories with mixed random clips and happy vibes"
